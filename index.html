IT Support Ticket Handling System
Overview

An intelligent end-to-end IT support automation that handles ticket intake, AI-powered classification, automated resolution, and Slack-based escalation — with user notifications at every stage of the process.

Problem

IT support teams faced high volumes of repetitive tickets, slow response times, and inconsistent communication with end users — creating frustration and operational inefficiency.

Solution

Built a multi-branch n8n workflow: tickets submitted via form are classified by Gemini AI, matched against a resolution database, and either auto-resolved with an email — or escalated to a Slack channel. When a specialist responds in Slack, the system automatically notifies the user and closes the ticket in Google Sheets.

Outcome

Routine ticket resolution time dropped from 3–4 hours to under 30 seconds, while manual ticket routing was eliminated entirely for known issue categories. Staff saved approximately 2–3 hours daily previously spent on ticket triage.

n8n Gemini AI Slack API Gmail Google Sheets JavaScript

Automated Lead Generation System
Overview

A fully automated lead generation pipeline that extracts targeted business data from Google Maps based on industry and location inputs — delivering clean, structured prospect lists on demand.

Problem

Sales and business development teams were spending hours manually researching and compiling prospect lists — a slow, error-prone, and unscalable process that pulled focus from actual selling.

Solution

Built an n8n workflow that accepts target inputs, queries Google Maps via HTTP requests, processes the results through a Gemini AI agent for data extraction and enrichment, and automatically appends structured lead data to a Google Sheet — ready for immediate outreach.

Outcome

Prospect list build time was reduced from hours of manual work to under 5 minutes per run. Manual copy-paste research was eliminated entirely, while each workflow execution generated 20–50 structured lead records with consistent data quality.

WhatsApp AI Chatbot
Overview

A conversational AI chatbot integrated with the WhatsApp Business API that handles inbound customer messages, delivers intelligent responses, and maintains conversation context across sessions — operating continuously without human input.

Problem

Businesses were missing customer enquiries outside business hours and spending excessive time responding to repetitive questions that did not require human intervention — leading to delayed responses and lost leads.

Solution

Developed a WhatsApp chatbot that triggers on every incoming message, processes it through an AI chatbot agent powered by OpenAI with persistent conversation memory, and sends contextually accurate responses — available around the clock with no manual effort required.

Outcome

Average response time dropped from 12–18 hours to under 10 seconds, while after-hours enquiry capture increased to 100% and routine customer enquiry handling time was reduced by approximately 80%.
